Fonts shipped with ThreadForge (served from our own origin; the Content-Security-Policy allows fonts from 'self' only).

Carlito  - metric-compatible with Calibri   (SIL Open Font License 1.1)  LICENSE-carlito.txt
Caladea  - metric-compatible with Cambria   (SIL Open Font License 1.1)  LICENSE-caladea.txt
Arimo    - metric-compatible with Arial     (SIL Open Font License 1.1)  LICENSE-arimo.txt
Tinos    - metric-compatible with Times New Roman (SIL OFL 1.1)          LICENSE-tinos.txt
Cousine  - metric-compatible with Courier New (SIL OFL 1.1)              LICENSE-cousine.txt

Source: the @fontsource packages (npm), latin subset, weights 400 and 700, normal and italic.
Characters outside the Latin subset use the system font. Documents keep a font KEY (sans, serif, mono,
calibri, cambria, arial, times_new_roman, courier_new), never a font name or URL.
